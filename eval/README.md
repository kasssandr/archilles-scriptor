# Golden volumes and the public/local split

`golden/` is committed and copyright-free: pages we authored ourselves.
`golden-local/` is gitignored: real volumes (Zuckerman 1972, Baynes, Snell,
the Oxford Handbook paragraph) are under copyright — they never enter the
repository, in line with the S2 legal caveat. A published benchmark set
needs public-domain volumes or a cleared excerpt solution first.

`outputs/` holds captured candidate outputs (`<volume>/<tool>.md`), produced
by the commands in `CANDIDATES.md`. `BENCHMARK-draft.md` is generated by
`scriptor eval suite` and stays local until publication (benchmark embargo).
